# Hyperarid Rainfall Emergent Constraints

This repository contains the manuscript, supplementary data, and figures for the paper:

**"Apparent emergent constraints on hyperarid extreme rainfall arise from predictand construction, sampling variability and post-processing"**

**Authors:** Mohib Ullah, Adam Fenech, Dan Scott, Xander Wang, Ross Gordon  
**Journal:** Scientific Reports  
**DOI:** [To be assigned upon publication]

---

## Overview

This study examines why plausible observable predictors fail to constrain projected extreme rainfall at the scale of a hyperarid country. We analyze the effects of predictand construction, sampling variability, and post-processing on emergent constraints for hyperarid extreme rainfall projections.

## Key Findings

1. **Predictand Construction**: The way extreme rainfall metrics are constructed significantly affects constraint performance
2. **Sampling Variability**: Limited observational records in hyperarid regions introduce substantial uncertainty
3. **Post-processing Effects**: Statistical downscaling and bias correction methods can alter constraint relationships
